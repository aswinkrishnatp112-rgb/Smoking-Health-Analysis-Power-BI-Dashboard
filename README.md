# 🚬 Smoking & Health Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Smoking & Health Analysis** project is an interactive data visualization dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. The project explores the relationship between smoking habits, patient demographics, and different health conditions.

The dashboard helps analyze smoking patterns, patient health indicators, and potential health risks through interactive charts and visual reports.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the distribution of smokers and non-smokers.
- Understand smoking patterns across different age groups.
- Explore the relationship between smoking status and health conditions.
- Analyze blood pressure risk categories among patients.
- Examine BMI categories and their distribution.
- Investigate diagnosed organ categories and associated health conditions.
- Understand the relationship between smoking exposure and health outcomes.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data transformation and preparation.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Calculated measures and KPIs.
- **Data Analytics** – Exploring patterns and identifying trends.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### Page 1 – Patient Health Overview

Key performance indicators (KPIs):
- Total Number of Patients
- Total Current Smokers
- Patients with Recorded Organ Category
- Total Patients with Health Condition

Visualizations:
- Patient Distribution by Age Group
- Patients by Blood Pressure Risk Category
- Patient Distribution by BMI Category

### Page 2 – Smoking & Health Insights

Visualizations:
- Average Cigarettes Consumed Per Day by Smoking Status
- Diagnosed Organ vs Health Condition
- Distribution of Smoking Status
- Total Cholesterol by Blood Pressure Risk Category
- Patient Distribution by Age Group and Smoking Status
- Health Conditions by Smoking Exposure Levels

## 🔍 Key Analysis Areas

1. **Smoking Behaviour:** Understand smoking status and daily cigarette consumption.
2. **Age-Based Analysis:** Examine patient distribution across different age groups.
3. **Health Risk Analysis:** Explore BMI, blood pressure, and cholesterol indicators.
4. **Organ Health Analysis:** Investigate diagnosed organs and associated health conditions.
5. **Smoking Exposure:** Explore how different smoking exposure levels relate to reported health conditions.
